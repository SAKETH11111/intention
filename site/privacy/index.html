Intention privacy policy

Effective October 11, 2026. This website is static.

What we collect

Nothing from this website. It sets no cookies, loads no tracking scripts and makes no requests to other services. The waitlist form sends nothing and stores nothing: submitting it only shows a thank-you in your browser.

The Mac alpha turns what you dictate into text on your Mac. It never sends a message for you.

How we use it

Only to run Intention. We don't sell data or use it for ads.

Deleting your data

This website keeps none, not even in your own browser.
