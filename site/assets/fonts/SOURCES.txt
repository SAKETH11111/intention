Fonts for the app windows on the home page (windows.css).
Each is a Latin subset (U+0020-007E, U+00A0-00FF and common punctuation) made
with fontTools and saved as WOFF2.

GoogleSansFlex-Latin.woff2
  Google Sans Flex from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/googlesansflex ,
  instanced at ROND 0, slnt 0, wdth 100, opsz 14, keeping weights 400 to 700.
  License: OFL-GoogleSansFlex.txt (no Reserved Font Name); trademarks:
  TRADEMARKS-GoogleSansFlex.md.

Roboto-Latin.woff2
  Roboto from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/roboto , instanced
  at wdth 100, keeping weights 400 to 700. License: OFL-Roboto.txt (no Reserved
  Font Name).
